Empowering Communities Through Housing

We believe everyone deserves a safe, affordable place to call home. Our mission is to provide pathways to homeownership for underserved communities.

Explore Our Programs

Our Programs

First-Time Homebuyer Assistance

We help first-time homebuyers navigate the complex process of purchasing their first home with counseling, education, and financial assistance.

Down Payment Support

Our down payment assistance program provides qualified families with the funds needed to overcome the biggest barrier to homeownership.

Financial Education

We offer comprehensive financial literacy courses to help families build credit, manage debt, and prepare for sustainable homeownership.

Our Mission

The Adelante Story Foundation is dedicated to breaking down barriers to homeownership and creating thriving communities. Through innovative programs, partnerships, and advocacy, we're building a future where everyone has the opportunity to own their home and build generational wealth.
