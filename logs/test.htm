Housing Affordability Resources

Your journey to homeownership starts with the right information and support. We've gathered essential resources to help you navigate down payment assistance programs, understand your options, and connect with trusted advisors who can guide you forward - ¡Adelante!

Mortgage Programs

FHA Loans - Government-backed loans requiring as little as 3.5% down with flexible credit requirements, ideal for first-time buyers

VA Loans - Zero down payment loans for active duty military, veterans, and eligible spouses with no mortgage insurance required

USDA Loans - Zero down payment loans for eligible rural and suburban areas with income limits, designed for low-to-moderate income buyers

Conventional Loans - Traditional mortgages not backed by government, typically requiring 3-20% down payment with competitive rates for strong credit

Down Payment Assistance Programs

Home Plus Arizona - Statewide program offering up to 4% assistance for down payment and closing costs

Arizona Is Home - State program providing 3-7% assistance for first-time buyers at or below 120% AMI

WISH Grant Program - 4:1 matching grant up to $32,099 for income-qualified first-time buyers

Home in Five Advantage - Up to 5% assistance (6% for teachers, first responders, military, and income-qualified buyers) in Maricopa County

Home in Five Platinum - Up to 4% forgivable second mortgage for first-time homebuyers in Maricopa County

Open Doors Down Payment Assistance - Up to $15,000 (10% of purchase price) for first-time buyers earning ≤80% AMI in Phoenix

Phoenix Housing Department - Additional homeownership opportunities and assistance programs
